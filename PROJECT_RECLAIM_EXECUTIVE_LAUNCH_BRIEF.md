# PROJECT RECLAIM
## Executive Launch Brief: Stewardship in Action

**Date:** August 15, 2026  
**Status:** Board & Agency Review Package (Draft)  
**Primary Context:** Mendocino & Lake County Pilot Operations  
**Vision Lead:** Tyler Morris  

---

## 1. The Core Mission
Project Reclaim is not a brochure; it is a physical-world operating system. We exist to solve three disconnected problems simultaneously: **wildfire risk, material waste, and economic exclusion.**

We reclaim forest health through fire mitigation, we reclaim wood as a community resource rather than waste, and most importantly, we reclaim lives by providing a stable, dignity-centered pathway for people who have been historically excluded from the economy—including veterans, people returning from incarceration, and those navigating housing or recovery barriers.

**The Foundational Principle:** The digital serves the physical. The physical proves the digital. The people heal the land.

---

## 2. The Two-Site Hybrid Model
To prove this vision, we are proposing a two-site pilot model in the Mendocino-Lake County region. This structure separates residential stability from industrial operations to ensure safety and focus.

### Site A: The Stewardship Campus (Residential)
*   **Target:** A rural vineyard or ranch property (e.g., Voigt Road Vineyard candidate).
*   **Function:** Stable housing, agriculture, land stewardship training, and future property rehabilitation.
*   **Goal:** Create a controlled, supportive environment for 12–20 participants to build a foundation of life skills and land-based learning.

### Site B: The Operations Hub (Industrial)
*   **Target:** A commercial/industrial site with shop and yard capacity (e.g., 3360 Old Highway 53 candidate).
*   **Function:** Wood reclamation, sawmilling, hand-tool carpentry, tool control, and field-school operations.
*   **Goal:** A hub for material recovery, local procurement, and supervised production where participants earn credentials through practical work.

---

## 3. The Pilot Cohort: "First Group"
We are starting with a disciplined, supervised cohort to ensure safety and measurable results.

*   **Size:** 12 residential participants + 8 community-based participants.
*   **Pathways:** 1-year foundational, 2-year advanced, and future 4-5 year apprenticeships.
*   **Focus:** Fire resilience, forestry, carpentry, disaster preparation, and property management.
*   **Human Development:** This is not charity; it is **capability creation**. Participants earn their way forward through a structured ladder: **Discover → Train → Certify → Work → Contribute → Advance.**

---

## 4. 60/90-Day Operational Roadmap
We are not asking for a leap of faith; we are asking for a disciplined mobilization.

### Phase 1: Mobilization (Days 1–30)
*   **Governance:** Execute MOUs with Tribal boards, County planning, and agency partners (CAL FIRE, HUD).
*   **Property Diligence:** Finalize title, zoning, and fire/life-safety reviews for the two pilot sites.
*   **Safety & Intake:** Establish the Safety Manual, Participant Rights, and the Consent-First Intake flow.
*   **Initial Procurement:** Secure PPE, hand-tool kits, trauma/first-aid, and basic crew transport.

### Phase 2: Establishment (Days 31–60)
*   **Site Setup:** Deploy field offices, tool cribs, and secure material storage.
*   **Staffing:** Onboard qualified supervisors (Forestry, Carpentry, Safety) and referral coordinators.
*   **Referral Launch:** Open intake for the first cohort through veteran, housing, and justice-involved partners.
*   **Field School:** Begin supervised, low-risk stewardship work (defensible space, grounds maintenance).

### Phase 3: Production & Evidence (Days 61–90)
*   **Material Recovery:** Begin wood recovery and sawmilling operations at the Hub.
*   **Carpentry Training:** Launch the hand-tool craft shop and initial site-rehabilitation projects.
*   **Evidence Registry:** Activate the Pilot Command Board to track work orders, materials, and training hours.
*   **Board Review:** Present the first 90-day evidence packet to partners and funders for Stage 2 scaling.

## 5. Pilot Requirements & Procurement
Our startup capital model is based on **Capability, not Clutter**. We buy the platform, rent the specialized tools, and scale only when demand is proven.

*   **Total Planning Capital:** $4.34M (100-person scale model).
*   **Immediate Pilot Need:** $450k – $600k (focusing on PPE, transport, hand tools, and site control).
*   **Key Assets:** Crew trucks, dump trailers, portable sawmills, professional saw kits, and safety gear.
*   **Safety First:** No high-hazard equipment is operated without documented qualification and supervision.

---

## 6. Partnership & Governance
Project Reclaim is designed to be **Audit-Ready**. We are seeking formal partnerships, not just endorsements.

*   **Tribal Boards:** Collaboration on culturally grounded stewardship and community land restoration.
*   **Agencies (CAL FIRE/HUD):** Defining authorized fire-resilience scopes and housing-stability alignment.
*   **Justice Partners:** Lawful referral pathways for people seeking a transition in lieu of incarceration.
*   **Local Business:** Procurement-first relationship for equipment, maintenance, and supplies.

**Non-Negotiable:** We do not provide clinical care, therapy, or emergency response. We provide the physical-work pathway and coordinate with licensed professionals for everything else.

# Project Reclaim Startup Package Source Log

**Reference date:** August 12, 2026 (Pacific Time)

This log preserves source inputs for the non-binding 100-person startup equipment and supplies proposal. Public postings are **planning benchmarks only**, not local California quotes, commitments, or evidence that a named model is appropriate for a particular site.

| Input | Observed source detail | Use in proposal | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Heavy-duty work truck capability | Ford’s 2026 Super Duty page identifies F-250/F-350/F-450 commercial models, selected towing/payload capabilities, and build-and-price paths; public page pricing is configuration/location dependent. | Establishes category suitability and quote-first procurement path; does not set a final truck price. | Medium |
| Compact track loader benchmark | A dealer listing showed a new 2026 Bobcat T770 at **$92,513** advertised price, excluding taxes/title/licensing/dealer/setup/destination and subject to change. | One public benchmark for the base package’s loader allowance. | Medium |
| Chipper benchmark | Vermeer Used listed 2023 BC1000XL units at **$44,000**, **$38,500**, and **$23,999**. | Supports a used-equipment planning allowance; model condition, location, service history, and transport remain quote items. | Medium |
| Professional saw price band | Husqvarna’s professional-saw catalog displayed a price filter from **$460.00** through **$2,000.00** across 36 products. | Establishes a public manufacturer-catalog range; the package uses an explicit, conservative per-saw allowance rather than a claimed quote. | Medium |
| California chain-saw controls | California Title 8 §6283 addresses leg protection, chain brakes, operator instruction, daily inspection, fueling and start-distance controls. | Supports inclusion of individual chain-saw PPE, inspection kits, fire controls, and training—not a substitute for a site-specific safety plan. | High |
| Logging worksite controls | OSHA 1910.266 covers PPE, first-aid kits, seat belts, extinguishers, tools, machines, communication, training, and related logging operations. | Supports the base package’s first-aid, fire extinguisher, communication, vehicle, and equipment-control allowances. | High |
| Woodshop controls | California Title 8 Article 59 lists woodworking-machine and dust-collection safety provisions; OSHA flags woodworking machinery hazards and general-industry standards. | Supports staging a guarded, dust-controlled shop rather than buying unguarded machinery or treating shop buildout as incidental. | High |

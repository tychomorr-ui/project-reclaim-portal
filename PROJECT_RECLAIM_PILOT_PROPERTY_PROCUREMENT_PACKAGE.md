## Procurement decision rule

Project Reclaim should buy only after an authorized work scope, property or agency authority, qualified supervision, safety plan, total-cost comparison, and funding source are documented. The default decision is **buy the shared platform, rent or subcontract specialized capacity, and defer high-hazard or low-utilization equipment** until actual demand is demonstrated.

## Quote request templates

Every vendor request should include the intended duty cycle, site conditions, operator profile, service geography, warranty and maintenance expectations, delivery terms, training, financing or rental alternative, parts availability, resale assumptions, insurance requirements, and total cost of ownership. Request at least three comparable quotes where the market allows. A quote is not an award until the property, work scope, funding, safety, and authorization gates are complete.

For a **vehicle or trailer**, request payload, towing capacity, crew configuration, accessibility, braking, securement, service coverage, telematics, registration, delivery, and maintenance. For a **loader, chipper, tractor, or excavator**, request attachments, operating weight, guarding, operator training, inspection history, service records, transport, site limitations, rental comparison, and emergency shutoff features. For a **shop package**, request a layout, electrical load, guarding, ventilation, dust collection, noise, accessibility, instructor plan, maintenance, inspection, and local permitting review. For **PPE and field safety**, request task-specific components, sizing, replacement schedule, inspection, training, first-aid, communications, traffic control, hydration, sanitation, and heat or cold protection.

## Budget structure for the board

The board package should not blend property acquisition with equipment. Present the request as separate envelopes: property due diligence and control; habitability and code work; staff and qualified supervision; participant support and lawful wages; hand tools and PPE; vehicles and logistics; shop and materials; communications and evidence; insurance and legal/professional review; and contingency. This makes it possible to fund a safe first stage without implying that the full long-term network is already financed.

## Assumptions and exclusions

The package assumes a small first cohort and two-site planning model. It does not assume a property is secured, that a court or corrections agency has authorized a referral pathway, that Project Reclaim operates a clinical service, that any participant is deployable to an emergency, or that a map, listing, or public data source proves authority to work. It excludes land price, acquisition costs, rehabilitation, housing operations, payroll, benefits, taxes, insurance premiums, ongoing maintenance, licensed services, agency programs, environmental permits, public-land approvals, and financing costs until site-specific review exists.

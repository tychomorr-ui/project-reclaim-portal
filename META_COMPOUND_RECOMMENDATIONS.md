# Project Reclaim Meta-Compound Recommendations

**Status:** Prioritized planning roadmap for board and implementation review.  
**Prepared by:** Manus AI  
**Date:** August 15, 2026

## Strategic recommendation

The next phase should not attempt to turn every future idea into a live integration at once. The strongest sequence is to make Project Reclaim **operationally legible** first: every project has a scope, every participant-facing pathway has a safe boundary, every public claim has a source or pending state, every owner action has an authorization guard, and every partner conversation has a clear ask. The ecosystem can then expand without turning into a maze.

## Further product upgrades

The next application iteration should add a **pilot command board** that displays only records with clear status: proposed, authorized, in progress, evidence pending, reviewed, released, or closed. It should show the responsible owner, last review date, and next required action. This is more useful than adding more decorative pages because it turns the portal into an operational map.

A **participant portfolio view** should allow a person to see their own work records, training milestones, credential issuer, consent status, and exportable summary. It should separate private case or support information from the work portfolio and make every public-sharing decision explicit. The system should support revocation and correction requests rather than treating a published record as permanent.

A **property readiness scorecard** should track title, zoning, occupancy, utilities, fire, flood, structural, environmental, insurance, community, and program-authority gates. It should never output a single “approved property” score until every required professional review is complete. A board member should be able to see what is known, what is missing, who owns the next step, and what decision is blocked.

A **seasonal readiness board** should organize tasks by prevention, preparedness, response-support, and recovery. It should distinguish routine work from incident work and never imply that Project Reclaim can self-deploy. The map should display authorized work areas, partner work orders, and evidence state without exposing private participant or property data.

## Further partnership upgrades

Project Reclaim should prepare partner-specific one-page asks rather than one general partnership form. The county ask may involve property, planning, public works, and emergency coordination. CAL FIRE or forestry partners may be asked to define training and authorized fire-resilience scopes. HUD-related partners may be asked to explore housing and community-development alignment. Tribal EcoRestoration Alliance may be asked to explore culturally grounded stewardship and community benefit. Probation, parole, corrections, and courts may be asked to explore lawful referral structures. Local vendors may be asked for comparable quotes, service agreements, training, and maintenance support.

The partnership registry should record **relationship type, requested contribution, decision owner, current status, data boundary, next meeting, and verification date**. This makes “connected to the ecosystem” mean something observable.

## Funding strategy

The funding story should begin with a pilot envelope rather than the entire long-term capital plan. Present a minimum viable safe pilot, a standard pilot, and a scale scenario. Each envelope should separate property, staffing, participant support, safety, equipment, professional services, evidence, and contingency. The board should be shown which costs are one-time, recurring, property-specific, and conditional on authorization.

Potential funding narratives include hazard mitigation and resilience, workforce development, reentry and housing stability, rural economic development, wood and material recovery, local procurement, environmental restoration, and veterans’ transition. Eligibility must be confirmed for each specific funder; the portal should never imply that a program is eligible merely because its mission sounds aligned.

## Non-negotiable boundaries

Project Reclaim should not claim current residential openings, clinical care, emergency authority, court-approved alternative sentencing, registered apprenticeship status, agency endorsement, property control, deployed radio or LoRa capability, live NEXINUS or Xinus integration, or blockchain verification until the underlying authorization, partner, service, property, or technical evidence exists. These boundaries are not a limitation on the vision. They are what make the vision credible to a county, agency, Tribal partner, funder, or board.

## Recommended next three builds

The immediate next build should be the **authorized pilot command board**, followed by the **credential and portfolio registry**, followed by the **property readiness and procurement desk**. Together, these three upgrades would make the portal useful in a real board conversation: one can show the work, show the person’s capability record, and show exactly what is needed to secure and operate the property.

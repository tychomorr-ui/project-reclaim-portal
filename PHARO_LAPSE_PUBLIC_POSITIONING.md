# Pharo-Lapse: Public Positioning

**Status:** Canonical public boundary

Pharo-Lapse is an offline-capable, signed, store-and-forward communications research and transport layer within the broader Nexinus ecosystem.

At this stage, Pharo-Lapse is **a development and testing program**. It is **not a completed countywide public-safety network, replacement for emergency communications, or live government system without formal testing, safety review, authorization, and agency acceptance**.

The program may explore local message creation, deterministic serialization, signed envelopes, durable queues, peer-to-peer forwarding, delayed delivery, signed acknowledgments, deduplication, replay protection, audit records, and geographically distributed peers.

A cryptographic signature can help establish message authenticity and data integrity. It does not, by itself, prove that a real-world event occurred, that a field crew acted, that a public-safety outcome was achieved, or that a government agency has accepted the system.

Public materials must distinguish clearly between development, testing, configured delivery, acknowledged delivery, field evidence, independent review, and authorized operational deployment. No live integration or operational claim may be made without the corresponding evidence artifact and responsible authorization record.

Pharo-Lapse is intended to complement—not replace—established emergency communications, dispatch, public-safety, and government systems unless and until formal testing, safety review, authorization, and agency acceptance establish otherwise.

# Project Reclaim Portal Decisions

## Public Positioning

Project Reclaim is presented as a **physical-world initiative** for fire mitigation, wood reclamation, workforce development, and land restoration. The public portal positions technology as a coordination and evidence layer that serves the physical mission, rather than as the mission itself.

## Public Information Boundaries

The public registry and opportunities board are intentionally data-driven and initially empty. The portal does not fabricate active projects, job openings, inventory, impact metrics, endorsements, affiliations, partner participation, or field outcomes. Empty states explain the proposed schema and transparency rules instead.

## Partner Context

The partnership page can name **Cal Fire**, **HUD**, **Tribal Economic Restoration Alliance**, and county bodies as intended outreach audiences. Those names must never imply endorsement, participation, or affiliation until a relationship is confirmed.

The Tribal EcoRestoration Alliance’s public website describes a community-based intertribal nonprofit advancing ecology, economy, and culture through Indigenous-led stewardship, with training and stewardship programs. That context informs respectful language only; Project Reclaim does not claim a formal relationship.

## Connected Ecosystem

The exact public connected-network name is **NEXINUS**. **Root** is a connected initiative within that context. All future public labels, navigation, copy, and integrations must use **NEXINUS**—never "Nexus."

## Evidence and Source Materials

The supplied Project Reclaim executive summary is available as an orientation download. It is presented as a draft framework resource, not as evidence of field results. Claims about current operations must be supported by verified registry records and associated documentation.

## Deferred Delivery Detail

Direct third-party email delivery is intentionally deferred until the site owner explicitly asks to activate it. Contact and partnership submissions are stored in the protected owner dashboard, and the portal retains in-app owner alerts.

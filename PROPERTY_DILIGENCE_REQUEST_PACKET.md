## County requests

For Lake County, use the official GIS and Assessor–Recorder resources to request or verify parcel maps, zoning, flood and hazard layers, public permit indexes, recorded-document index access, and property-value or tax-status information. The county’s public GIS page identifies parcel zoning, flood zones, soils, and natural hazards as available map context.[4] The Assessor–Recorder page links to assessor information, parcel viewer, property values, recorder information, and the recorder’s search index.[5]

A county record may help answer “what does the county show?” It does not replace a title commitment, survey, inspection, insurance quote, or legal opinion.

## Professional requests

| Professional | Ask for | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Title company or real-estate counsel | Current title commitment, legal description, ownership, liens, easements, restrictions, taxes, and exceptions | Establishes whether the proposed transaction is legally understandable and what risks attach to the property |
| Building / land-use professional | Zoning and occupancy confirmation, permit history, code issues, change-of-use requirements, septic/water review | Establishes whether the intended residential, workshop, retail, training, or operations uses are allowed and what approvals are needed |
| Licensed inspector and safety professional | Structural, electrical, plumbing, roof, fire/life-safety, access, environmental, mold/asbestos, and hazard review as applicable | Establishes whether people can safely occupy or work on the site and what repairs may be required |
| Insurance broker | Property, general liability, workers’ compensation, auto, commercial, residential, and participant-related coverage quotes | Establishes whether the planned activity can be insured and at what cost |
| Lender or finance professional | Financing terms, down payment, debt service, seller financing terms, reserves, and underwriting conditions | Establishes whether the acquisition or rehabilitation is financially feasible |
